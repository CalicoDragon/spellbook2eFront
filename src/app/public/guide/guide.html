Welcome to Spellbook2e Guide!

Search, rank and save your favorite pathfinder spells!

Introduction

Welcome to spellbook2e, the web tool for all your spell searching needs! The reason why I made this website is, frankly, to use it myself. I love using Scryfall and I wanted to make a similar tool for pathfinder spells, as I always struggled to look for spells in the system with the current tools.

The main features of the website are the searchbar, and the ability to have your own personal data on your favorite spells!
For the searchbar, I made this guide page, it has many filters so I thought it was needed. Otherwise, It would be pretty much impossible to figure it out.
I took inspiration by all of those pathfinder2e guides online that rank features of the system and add a little bit of their opinion, so you can also do that here.
It is stored in the localStorage inside your browser, so only YOU have access to it. This also means that if you plan on using spellbook2e in a different browser or device you will have to export your data, which is explained at the end of the guide.

I hope you enjoy using spellbook2e as much as I enjoyed making it, if you have any suggestion or bug report, anything... let me know 😼

- aCalicoDragon

Name

Look for spells with a certain name by using the 'name:' keyword or by typing without a keyword.

Name searches are not case sensitive, and they also allow for partial searches.

name:"Acid Splash"

Will look for spells where their name include "Acid Splash"

name:Ac name:Sp

Will look for spells where their name include "Ac" and "Sp"

ac sp

No need for uppercase letters or writing the name: keyword!

Traits

Use the keywords 'trait:', 'tr:' or 't:' to look for spells with a certain trait. The trait must be entirely in lowercase.

Trait searches work by matching exactly to the name of the trait. Use the same keywords with an exclamation mark in front to look for spells that do not contain said trait.

trait:fire

Will look for spells with the fire trait

!t:mental

Will look for spells without the mental trait

Tradition

Use the keywords 'tradition:' or 'trad:' to look for spells with of a certain tradition. The tradition must be entirely in lowercase.

Tradition searches work by matching exactly to the name of the tradition. Use the same keywords with an exclamation mark in front to look for spells that are not part of said tradition.

tradition:arcane

Will look for spells in the arcane tradition.

trad:arcane trad:primal !trad:occult

Will look for spells that are arcane and primal but not occult

Rank

Use the keywords 'rank:', 'lvl:' or 'r:' to look for spells of a certain rank. The rank must be a number.

Rank searches work by matching exactly to the number of the rank. Use the same keywords with an exclamation mark in front to look for spells that are not of that rank. In addition, look for spells with a greater or lesser rank than the argument by adding either '>' or '<' before the keyword.

rank:1

Will look for 1st rank spells

!lvl:5

Will look for spells that are not rank 5.

<lvl:5

Will look for spells with a rank lesser than 5.

Actions

Use the keywords 'actions:' or 'a:' to look for spells that require a specific amount of actions. For the value of the keyword encapsulate it as "1 action" since the search uses spaces as a divider of filters.

Action searches work by matching exactly to the name and number of the action. Use the same keywords with an exclamation mark in front to look for spells that do not require that action.

actions:1

Will look for spells that require one action.

a:"1 to 3"

Will look for spells that require one to three actions.

a:"2 or 3"

Will look for spells that require two or three actions.

Save target

Use the keyword 'save:' to look for spells that target a certain save throw. The save must be entirely in lowercase.

Save throw searches work by matching exactly to the name of the save. Use the same keyword with an exclamation mark in front to look for spells that do not target that save.

save:fortitude

Will look for spells that require one action.

!save:will

Will look for spells that require one to three actions.

Range

Use the keyword 'range:' to look for spells that have a certain range. For the value of the keyword encapsulate it as "20 feet" since the search uses spaces as a divider of filters.

Range searches work by matching exactly to the name and number of the range. Use the same keyword with an exclamation mark in front to look for spells that do not have that range.

range:touch

Will look for spells that are touch range.

!range:"30 feet"

Will look for spells that aren't 30 feet.

Area type

Use the keyword 'area:' to look for spells of a specific area type. The area must be in lowercase.

Area searches work by matching exactly to the name of the area type. Use the same keyword with an exclamation mark in front to look for spells that do not have that area type. The area types are burst, cone, emanation and line.

area:burst

Will look for spells with a burst area type.

!area:cone

Will look for spells without a cone area type.

Duration

Use the keyword 'duration:' to look for spells that last for a certain duration. For the value of the keyword encapsulate it as "10 minutes" since the search uses spaces as a divider of filters.

Duration searches work by matching exactly to the name and number of the duration. Use the same keyword with an exclamation mark in front to look for spells that do not last that duration.

duration:"10 minutes"

Will look for spells that have a 10 minute duration.

!duration:"1 round"

Will look for spells that don't have a 1 round duration.

Is Sustained

Use the keyword 'sustained:' to look for spells can be either sustained or not. For this keyword the argument will have to be either 'true' or 'false', in lowercase.

sustained:true

Will look for spells that can be sustained.

sustained:false

Will look for spells that can't be sustained.

Target

Use the keyword 'target:' to look for spells that have a certain target. For the value of the keyword encapsulate it as "1 creature" since the search uses spaces as a divider of filters.

Target searches work by matching exactly to the name and number of the target. Use the same keyword with an exclamation mark in front to look for spells that do not have that target.

target:"1 creature"

Will look for spells that target a creature.

!target:"1 object"

Will look for spells that target an object.

Exporting and Importing data

You may want to export your descriptions, rankings, favorited and prepared spells. This is stored in the "localStorage" of your browser so there is no need for you to create an account.

By pressing the export button in the top bar in the main app, you can download this data to later import it with the button next to it. This is what you need to do if you are scared of losing the data, so, as a backup of sorts, or if you just want to share it with a friend.
