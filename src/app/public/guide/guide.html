Name

You can look up for spells with a certain name by using the name: keyword or by just typing without a keyword. Name searches are case insensitive so you don't have to worry about perfectly matching the name of a spell.

For the parameter, you can encapsulate the value with the use of name:"value" or simply just "value", this is useful as you may want a literal name without making two filters, as the search uses spaces as a way to separate filters

Name searches work making them match those words in that order anywhere in a spell name, this means you can write fragments of names and they search for anything that matches.

name:"Acid Splash"

Will look for spells where their name include "Acid Splash"

name:Ac name:Sp

Will look for spells where their name include "Ac" and "Sp"

ac sp

No need for uppercase letters or writing the name: keyword!

Traits

You can look up for spells with a certain trait by using the trait:, tr: or t: keywords. For this keyword, the trait will have to be entirely in lowercase.

Trait searches work by matching exactly the name of the trait, except you can also use the same keywords with an exclamation mark in front of the keyword to look for spells that do not contain said trait.

trait:fire

Will look for spells with the fire trait

!t:mental

Will look for spells without the mental trait

Tradition

You can look up spells that are inside a specific tradition by using the tradition: or trad: keywords. For this keyword, the trait will have to be entirely in lowercase.

Tradition searches work by matching exactly the name of the trait, except you can also use the same keywords with an exclamation mark in front of the keyword to look for spells that are not part of said tradition.

tradition:arcane

Will look for spells in the arcane tradition.

trad:arcane trad:primal !trad:occult

Will look for spells that are arcane and primal but not occult

Rank

You can look up spells by their rank using the rank:, lvl: or r: keywords. For the parameter, always include a number numerically.

Rank searches work by matching the number of the rank, except you can also use the same keywords with an exclamation mark in front of the keyword to look for spells that are not said rank.

rank:1

Will look for 1st rank spells

!lvl:5

Will look for spells that are not rank 5.

Actions

You can look up spells by the amount or type of actions they require by using the actions: or a: keywords. For the parameter, you can encapsulate the value with the use of actions:"value" for the ones that need spaces in their value, as the search uses spaces as a way to separate filters.

Action searches work by matching the literal name of the actions, except you can also use the same keywords with an exclamation mark in front of the keyword to look for spells that do not use said actions.

actions:1

Will look for spells that require one action.

a:"1 to 3"

Will look for spells that require one to three actions.

Examples

trad:arcane lvl:2 trait:attack

Level 3 arcane caster looking for new toys to use with sure strike? Check these out!

trad:primal actions:1

Primal caster looking for something to fill out that remaining action?

trad:occult !trait:mental

Maybe you are just tired of mindless creatures!

a:"1 to 3" save:fortitude

Maybe preparing some action flexibility would be nice to have with the uncertainty of the session.

Exporting and Importing data

You may want to export your descriptions, rankings, favorited and prepared spells. This is stored in the "localStorage" of your browser so there is no need for you to create an account.

By pressing the export button in the top bar in the main app, you can download this data to later import it with the button next to it. This is what you need to do if you are scared of losing the data, so, as a backup of sorts, or if you just want to share it with a friend.
